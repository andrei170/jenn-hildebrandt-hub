01

Research Snapshot

5 structural gaps in the CBA funnel

The Five Gaps

  • 63K IG funnel has no owned home. Site still shows 2023 CBA branding. If Meta throttles the account, the audience goes dark with no backup.
  • DM-based nurture does not scale. Manual replies cap lead throughput at personal bandwidth. At 63K followers, that ceiling hits fast.
  • $48M claim has no on-site proof anchor. Strongest marketing claim only appears in IG captions. No case-study page with student stories and timelines.
  • No public opt-in page. Cannot drive paid ad traffic anywhere outside IG. No retargeting outside Meta walls.
  • Brand architecture competes with itself. CBA, That Freedom Life, and personal-name positioning all surface. Cold traffic cannot tell which is the flagship.

Highest-Leverage Fix

Build a modern landing page with email capture. Set up automated nurture after the DM opt-in. Publish a case-study page anchoring the $48M claim. Consolidate under CBA as the primary brand.

Sales-skill forward, not mindset-forward. Lead with verifiable corporate resume. Avoid "manifest," "abundance," "elevated," "boss babe," "aligned." Specific numbers with context.

10-Email Nurture Sequence

Blueprint opt-in to application, 25-day drip

Sequence Arc

  • Day 0 - Welcome: why the resume matters, blueprint delivery
  • Day 2 - The DM framework: 5 steps to closing in conversations
  • Day 4 - "I cannot afford it": the one question that reframes the objection
  • Day 7 - Rachel's story: $800/mo to $18K/mo in 5 months
  • Day 10 - Why posting more will not fix your income (the conversion math)
  • Day 13 - Pricing psychology: tripled the price, got more clients
  • Day 16 - Origin story: leaving the corporate conference room
  • Day 19 - Inside CBA: full program breakdown, tiers, what is included
  • Day 22 - The $200K mistake: seven offers, none selling. Simplicity won.
  • Day 25 - Final CTA: three options (apply, keep learning, or pass)

Design

150-300 words per email. Written from Jenn to her audience. Story-based, value-first, no hard pitch until Day 19. Corporate-credentialed tone throughout.
