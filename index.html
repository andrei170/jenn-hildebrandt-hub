Compound Pickup · Install
03

The Blueprint + FB Group Have No Email Nurture Layer

The FREE 6+7 Figure PASSIVE Blueprint is a verified high-intent lead magnet (women DM "READY" specifically to receive it). The Confidence Babe Academy FB Group is an active student community. And there's no observable email nurture sequence routing the high-intent leads from Blueprint-receipt into the paid program ladder, nor any observable email-capture bridge routing FB Group members into the email list. Industry-norm warm-to-paid conversion is 2-5% on a lead magnet of this intensity once the nurture is layered in. The cost of generating each lead is already paid. Only the routing layer is missing.

Cost of Waiting

~$2.5-10K/mo in warm-audience-to-paid conversions on existing FREE Blueprint volume + FB Group community that currently don't route through an email nurture. ~$30-120K/yr compounding as Blueprint opt-ins and Group member growth continues.

12 months from now ·~$30-120K never collected. Plus every Blueprint download and every Group member that doesn't route through an email is a permanent loss of warm-audience inventory.

Fix · 10-touch Blueprint nurture sequence (Day 1 delivery + welcome, Days 2-7 chapter-mapped methodology breakdowns, Days 8-10 tier-routed CTAs). FB Group pinned email-capture bridge with Group-exclusive opt-in. Day 4-10 install. Year-1 floor $30K-$120K direct.

No Hero VSL Pre-Framing The $48M Methodology

With a hero VSL pre-framing the methodology in the founder's voice, the floor moves to 3-5% on coaching traffic of this register.

Cost of Waiting

~$2.5-8K/mo in cold-traffic conversion that runs at 1-2% floor instead of 3-5% once the hero VSL is filmed and embedded. ~$30-100K/yr in incremental revenue from the same audience traffic + retention lift on the program pages as warm prospects pre-framed by the VSL convert at higher rates.

12 months from now ·~$30-100K never collected. Plus every cohort, course, and 1:1 landing has the same VSL gap.

Fix · Hero VSL on consolidated homepage (4-7 min, corporate-to-$48M arc + methodology overview + tier-routed CTA). Per-program VSLs (2-3 min each) on course, cohort, 1:1 landings. Day 7-21 record + embed. Year-1 floor $30K-$100K direct.
