Phase 3

Content Strategy · Jenn Hildebrandt / Confidence Babe Academy

Prepared 2026-05-12 by AG1 Consulting. Distribution-engine plan for a 63K IG operator with a $48M closed-revenue claim and a women-only high-ticket-sales-coaching ICP. LinkedIn long-form is the load-bearing layer (the female-corporate-to-coach ICP screens on LinkedIn). IG continues as the flagship discovery surface. YouTube long-form is the Year-2 compound layer. Threads stays as a connective tissue surface, not a primary growth channel.

1. Voice Tokens

Female-founder professional. Confident, structured, soft-life accent kept subtle. NOT soft-feminine pander. NOT operator-bro cadence.

Tokens to mirror from Jenn's existing surfaces:

  • "Soft Life Income" (positional descriptor, not adjective-stack)
  • "High Ticket" (used as a category, not a hype word)
  • "7 Figure Business Owner / Coach" (own-claim register)
  • "Escape The Matrix" (lifestyle hook, used sparingly)
  • "Helping You Start + Scale Online" (active-construction service framing)
  • "$48M Sold in 2025" (load-bearing proof claim)

Tokens to avoid (off-brand for her register):

  • "Babe / Queen / Boss-babe / Divine / Manifest" overused (the Academy uses "Babe" once, in the brand name; over-using it in content reads pander)
  • "Hustle / Grind / Pressure" (Soft Life register specifically rejects this framing)
  • "Game-changer / Revolutionary / Unlock your potential / Transform your life" (banned AI slop)
  • Em-dashes (banned per copy rules)

2. Channel Mix + Allocation

Cadence: 3-5x/week. 80% long-form text posts, 20% carousel essays. Register: Female-founder professional. Methodology breakdowns, corporate-to-coach transition story, $48M operator math, student case studies (post-Day-30 when real testimonials are sourceable), Soft-Life-Income lifestyle vignettes integrated with the operator math, not separate from it.   4. "I sold $48M last year. The framework is 6 steps. Step 1 is the one most women skip."

  6. "The 6+7 Figure Passive Blueprint isn't passive. It's leveraged. Here's the distinction that breaks open women's high-ticket sales careers."
  7. "Three things that work in women-led high-ticket sales that the bro-coaching world won't tell you."
  8. "The ADP-to-CEO transition I made in 2021. What I'd do differently if I started CBA today."

Source IP: All written from existing reel transcripts plus the methodology curriculum plus Jenn's documented career stack. No new content production required, just translation into LinkedIn-native long-form.

Instagram (flagship discovery · maintain cadence)

Cadence: 5-7x/week mix of reels, carousels, stories, live. Register: Existing register holds. Reels open with the methodology hook + soft-life-income lifestyle accent. The 63K audience is the existing flywheel; this channel doesn't need restructuring, it needs the distribution layer to compound around it. Adjustment: Bio CTA shifts from "DM READY" only → "DM READY OR opt in at [hub URL]" once the public commerce surface is live (Bottleneck #2 fix).

Threads (connective tissue · maintain)

Cadence: 2-3x/week. Repurpose LinkedIn long-form into Threads-native shorter snippets. Register: Same as LinkedIn but condensed. Growth target: 3.6K → 10K-15K in Year-1 as a side effect of the LinkedIn cadence. Not a primary growth target.

YouTube long-form (Year-2 layer)

Cadence at launch: 1-2x/week, 12-20 min episodes. Format: Methodology breakdowns + student case studies + corporate-to-coach transition deep-dives. Each episode mapped to a tier-routed CTA on the offer ladder. Launch timing: Month 4-6 after Year-1 infrastructure is live (public commerce surface + email nurture + LinkedIn cadence compounding).

Facebook Page + Group (community surface · maintain + bridge)

Cadence: 2-3x/week in the Group, 1-2x/week on the Page. Adjustment: Add the pinned email-capture bridge in the Group (per Bottleneck #3). Group continues as community surface; email infrastructure becomes the conversion engine.

3. Content Pillars

Four pillars rotate weekly across LinkedIn + IG (with platform-specific cadence). Each pillar is rooted in Jenn's verified credibility stack, not invented positioning.

Pillar 1 · Corporate-To-Coach Transition Methodology

The arc from ADP / LocaliQ / Funnel Pop Media into building $48M closed revenue. Decade-of-corporate-sales-and-HR translated into curriculum for women considering the leap. This pillar carries the credibility weight. Posts: career-stack stories, transition decision points, what the corporate track teaches that coaching alone doesn't, why the corporate-to-coach prospect should not skip the foundation.

Pillar 2 · The 6+7 Figure Passive Blueprint (Methodology Curriculum)

Direct translation of the FREE Blueprint asset into LinkedIn long-form. Each post unpacks one component of the methodology, opens with a hook, closes with a tier-routed CTA. The Blueprint becomes the engine for top-of-funnel; LinkedIn long-form becomes the warm-up surface that turns Blueprint opt-ins into paid enrollments.

Pillar 3 · Soft-Life-Income Lifestyle Integration

The lifestyle accent paired with the operator math. NOT lifestyle alone. Posts: how the $48M revenue funded the soft-life lifestyle, how to architect a business that doesn't require 60-hour weeks, how the "passive" component of the methodology shows up in actual lived experience. This pillar carries the brand-pull. Without operator math underneath, the soft-life register reads as fluff. With operator math underneath, it reads as differentiator.

Pillar 4 · Student Results + Case Studies (post Day-30)

Real testimonials with named attribution. Each post is one student's story: starting point, methodology applied, outcome. This pillar can't ship in Year-1 Week 1 (real testimonials need to be sourced from Jenn's existing client roster on Day 1 of install). It ships from Week 4 onwards once the case-study capture process is set up.

4. Conversion Path · How Distribution Routes Into The Funnel

Every long-form post on LinkedIn + IG + Threads + YouTube closes with one of three CTAs (rotated by pillar + audience-stage):

  • Top of funnel: Opt in for the FREE 6+7 Figure PASSIVE Blueprint → email nurture → tier-routed CTA
  • Mid funnel: Join the cohort / self-paced course (mid-tier ladder offer, sub-$500 to low-four-figure)
  • Bottom funnel: Application Typeform for 1:1 / mastermind (high-tier, application-only)

The LinkedIn engine compounds the audience that converts at the corporate-to-coach ICP. The IG engine maintains the broader audience flow. The email engine converts both into paid enrollments via the warm-audience nurture install.

6. Distribution Calibration · Why LinkedIn Beats More IG

Adding more IG to a 63K IG audience that already converts inside the existing IG funnel hits diminishing returns. The marginal IG follower converts at a lower rate than the marginal LinkedIn follower in this ICP.

The corporate-to-coach prospect: - Sees Jenn on IG, gets interested - Searches "Jenn Hildebrandt" on Google or LinkedIn before paying - Reads her on LinkedIn for trust signals (real career stack, methodology articulated in long-form, professional register) - Converts

If the LinkedIn surface is underweight, the conversion breaks at step 3. The 63K IG audience compounds is already saturated; LinkedIn is the unlock layer.

7. Year-1 Quarterly Plan

Q1 · Foundation (Months 1-3)

  • LinkedIn cadence locks at 3x/week minimum (operator-direct methodology + corporate-to-coach pillars)
  • Blueprint email nurture sequence live (10 touches)
  • Public commerce surface live with visible tier ladder + hero VSL stub
  • FB Group → email bridge live
  • IG cadence unchanged

Q2 · Compound (Months 4-6)

  • LinkedIn cadence to 4-5x/week, student-results pillar activates as real testimonials get sourced
  • Hero VSL filmed and embedded
  • YouTube long-form pilot launches (1x/week)
  • Email list grows from blueprint opt-ins + FB Group bridge + LinkedIn-sourced opt-ins compounding

Q3-Q4 · Scale (Months 7-12)

  • LinkedIn 5x/week sustained
  • YouTube 2x/week sustained
  • Year-2 podcast asset scoped (Bottleneck #5 fix prepped for Year-2)
  • Affiliate distribution layer compounds (the Blueprint methodology naturally generates affiliate-marketing opportunities for top-cohort grads, which is consistent with Jenn's existing "passive income" framing)

8. Off-Limits Content (Compliance + Brand)

  • No specific income guarantees. Even when student-results land, frame as "what student X achieved" not "what every student will achieve."
  • No "make $48M like Jenn" implied. The $48M is Jenn's personal closing track record. Student-result language must reference what students achieved, with FTC disclaimer applied.
  • No solo-bro pressure tactics. Soft-life register is the brand promise. Pressure-cooker enrollment language is off-brand.
  • No payment terms in cold content. Pricing surfaces only after the prospect engages with the funnel.

9. Source IP Inventory (No New Production Required Year-1)

  • Existing IG reel catalogue (estimated 100s of reels at 63K following)
  • The 6+7 Figure PASSIVE Blueprint asset (already produced)
  • Corporate career stack (ADP / LocaliQ / Funnel Pop Media / Verizon / Ascentis) ready for long-form translation
  • $48M closed revenue methodology curriculum
  • FB Group community + active student roster (for case studies Day-30 onwards)

Year-1 distribution is 90% repurposing existing IP into LinkedIn-native long-form + email infrastructure. New production (hero VSL, YouTube pilot) is layered in Q2+ once the foundation compounds.
