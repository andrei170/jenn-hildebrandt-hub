Funnel Blueprint · Jenn Hildebrandt / Confidence Babe Academy

Prepared 2026-05-12 by AG1 Consulting. Offer-ladder architecture for a $48M operator with a 63K IG audience and a women-only high-ticket sales coaching brand. Built to convert the existing audience volume into paid enrollments at every tier, with the LinkedIn distribution engine (Bottleneck #1) feeding the top of funnel and the warm-audience email nurture (Bottleneck #3) routing high-intent leads into the right tier.

The single-channel-dependent finding

Even with site state unverified, the verifiable architecture currently routes the 63K IG audience primarily through the DM funnel into the Blueprint. The Blueprint is a high-intent lead magnet routed through a DM gate, which means the conversion path is single-channel + single-surface + non-compounding. That's the architectural gap regardless of site state.

2. Target State Map (Year-1 Install)

Tier 0 · Top Of Funnel · FREE 6+7 Figure PASSIVE Blueprint

Format: Email-gated landing page (in addition to existing DM trigger word route). Conversion path: Landing → email capture → instant delivery → 10-touch nurture sequence → tier-routed CTAs. Why dual-rail: Keep the DM route live for IG-native flow. Add the landing route to capture search-arrival, LinkedIn-sourced, and FB Group-sourced leads. Email-list ownership instead of platform-dependent DM ownership. Public surface: Hero landing page section + dedicated /blueprint URL on the brand domain.

Tier 1 · Entry · Signature Self-Paced Course

Format: Sub-$500 self-paced course. Methodology curriculum core, delivered via Skool or Teachable. Tier intent: First paid touchpoint. Validates intent + warm-up for higher tiers. Public surface: /course landing page with offer-tier overview + Stripe checkout. VSL pre-frame (2-3 min). Suggested pricing range (Andrei's lift suggestion, Jenn confirms): $297-$497. Public price visible.

Tier 2 · Mid · Group Cohort / Quarterly Mastermind

Format: Low-four-figure to $2K group cohort, capped at 15-30 seats per quarter. 6-8 week curriculum + community + group coaching. Tier intent: The graduate-from-course bucket plus the corporate-to-coach prospect ready for community + coaching. Public surface: /cohort landing page with cohort calendar, application Typeform, social proof + named testimonials. Suggested pricing range: $1,500-$3,000. Public price visible.

Tier 3 · High · 1:1 Mentorship / Premium Mastermind

Format: $5K-$15K 1:1 or premium small-group mastermind (3-6 month engagement). Tier intent: The serious high-ticket-operator prospect ready for 1:1 access. Public surface: /apply Typeform, application-only, price confirmed at intake. Suggested pricing range: $5,000-$15,000. Public price NOT visible (application-only).

Tier 4 (optional) · Affiliate / Done-With-You Track

Format: High-ticket affiliate marketing track. Likely already in Jenn's curriculum given the "6+7 Figure PASSIVE Blueprint" framing and the "Escape the Matrix" lifestyle positioning. Tier intent: Top-cohort graduates monetise the methodology by reselling Jenn's affiliate offers. Keeps the LTV climbing post-cohort. Public surface: /partnership or /affiliate page, application-only. Note: Existing infrastructure verification needed. Jenn confirms what's live and what gets layered in Day 1.

9. Risk + Compliance

  • FTC scope: All income-claim copy across hub + landing + email + VSL must include the results-not-typical disclaimer pattern. Specific $48M is Jenn's own track record, not a student promise.
  • Single-channel risk mitigation: Year-1 plan deliberately moves the audience-to-paid path off DM-only architecture onto a brand-controlled email list. Meta account-action remains a risk but stops being existential.
  • Brand-consolidation: "That Freedom Life" FB Page alias should be renamed to Confidence Babe Academy OR retired with a redirect, to consolidate brand search.
  • Affiliate compliance: If affiliate marketing is part of the methodology stack, all affiliate copy must include affiliate disclosure per FTC Endorsement Guides.
