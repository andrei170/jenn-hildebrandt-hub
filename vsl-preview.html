Prepared 2026-05-12 by AG1 Consulting. Short hero VSL preview (3-4 minutes) designed as the homepage opener once the public commerce surface is restored or rebuilt. Pre-frames the corporate-to-$48M methodology arc, the operating-system + offer-ladder + distribution layer, and the tier-routed CTA. This is a preview script only. Final hero VSL is filmed Day 14-21 once brand + tier copy is locked.

Length: 3-4 minutes Register: Female-founder professional. Soft-life accent integrated with operator math. Setting: Brand-aligned filming location (home office / studio). Lighting warm, soft. Wardrobe in brand palette (rose / cream / navy if Jenn confirms the palette; otherwise her existing brand look). Visual: Static talking-head with light B-roll cutaway to methodology framework graphics (Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3 visual asset overlays).

Hook (0:00 - 0:15)

I closed $48M in 2025. Before I built Confidence Babe Academy, I spent a decade in corporate sales, HR, and digital marketing at ADP, LocaliQ, and Funnel Pop Media. The corporate stack is the foundation under everything I teach.

In the next four minutes, I'll show you the operating system underneath the $48M and the three steps most women coaches miss.

Setup · Why The Methodology Exists (0:15 - 0:45)

Most women high-ticket sales coaches start with the offer. They pick a $5K mastermind or a $2K cohort, build a funnel, write a sales script, and run ads. Some of them make money. But the business runs them. The hours stack. The income climbs but the cost of the income climbs faster. Soft Life vanishes.

I built Confidence Babe Academy to fix that. The methodology isn't about selling more. It's about architecting a business that funds the life you want before you ever launch the offer.

Step 1 · The Operating System Layer (0:45 - 1:30)

Step 1 is the operating system. Not the offer. Not the funnel. The operating system underneath all of it.

Three pieces. Capacity, pricing, and time. How many clients can you actually serve at the quality your reputation demands. What's the price point that funds the life and respects the work. How many hours per week is sustainable.

Most coaches answer these questions last. They pick the offer first, then back into capacity and pricing. That's the trap. Once you commit to an offer at a price point, you've decided the capacity and the hours, even if you haven't admitted it to yourself.

The Blueprint runs Step 1 first because every downstream decision compounds on it. Get Step 1 right and the rest of the methodology lands. Get it wrong and the business runs you.

Step 2 · The Offer Ladder Shape (1:30 - 2:15)

Step 2 is the offer ladder. Three tiers minimum, all visible, prices public except the top tier.

Tier 1 is the entry. Sub-$500 self-paced course. Validates intent and warms up for the cohort.

Tier 2 is the cohort. Low-four-figure to $3,000 group program. Six to eight weeks with community and live coaching. This is where most of the revenue lives.

Tier 3 is the 1:1 or premium mastermind. $5K to $15K, application-only.

Each tier feeds the next. Without all three visible, the audience can't self-select. The audience routes itself into the right tier, the close ratio climbs, the LTV compounds.

Step 3 · The Distribution Layer (2:15 - 3:00)

Step 3 is distribution. Specifically, the channel that screens for the women-in-corporate ICP.

LinkedIn. The six-figure HR professional, the account executive, the mid-level manager considering the leap to coaching. They scroll Instagram, get interested, then go to LinkedIn to verify. If LinkedIn is light, the conversion breaks at the credibility gate.

The fix isn't more Instagram. The fix is a LinkedIn long-form cadence in your voice, three to five posts a week, methodology from existing reel IP, corporate-to-coach transition stories, operator math. No new content production. Just translation into LinkedIn-native long-form.

That's the distribution unlock. The audience on Instagram stays the same. The conversion compounds because LinkedIn finally exists at the cadence the ICP screens at.

Close · The Three Paths Inside CBA (3:00 - 3:45)

The full methodology runs through six steps. We've covered three.

Three paths forward inside Confidence Babe Academy.

If you're early-stage and want to ground the operating system before you scale, the self-paced course is the start. Tier 1.

If you're past the first $5K month and ready for the cohort with community and live coaching, the cohort fills quarterly. Tier 2.

If you're already at $10K-$30K months and want 1:1 access to the full methodology, the 1:1 track is application-only. Tier 3.

Pick the tier that matches where you are. Or hit the contact link and tell me where you're at. I read every message.

Glad you're here.
