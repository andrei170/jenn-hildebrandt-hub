Foundation

Copy Strategy · Jenn Hildebrandt / Confidence Babe Academy

Prepared 2026-05-12 by AG1 Consulting. Voice-register match for a female-founder professional running a women-only high-ticket sales coaching brand with a $48M closed-revenue claim and a Soft-Life-Income positioning layer. This document codifies the voice, the banned phrases, and the headline + opener bank for all prospect-facing surfaces.

1. Voice Register

Primary register: Female-founder professional. Confident, structured, results-anchored. Soft-life accent kept subtle, paired with operator math so the lifestyle framing is differentiator (not fluff).

Cadence tokens:

  • Active verbs ("built," "closed," "scaled," "ran")
  • Comma-rhythm sentence structure with one strong clause + one calibrating clause
  • Single-clause hooks (3-7 words) that anchor the operator claim
  • Specific numbers used as anchors ($48M, 63K, 10-year corporate track, 6+7 Figure)
  • Soft-life accent applied to lifestyle outcome, NOT to the methodology itself

Reference voice match: Read like Jenn would write her own LinkedIn long-form if she wrote it weekly. Read like a methodology-rich operator who also runs a soft-life business. Not "manifestation coach" voice. Not "bro-closer hard-pitch" voice. The middle register where ADP-trained operator meets confident female founder.

2. Banned Phrases (Hard Filter)

AI-slop (universal ban):

  • "g a m e - c h a n g e r" (any spacing), "r e v o l u t i o n a r y" (any spacing)
  • "u n l o c k your potential," "t r a n s f o r m your life"
  • "in today's fast-paced world," "navigate the complexities," "delve into"
  • "it's important to note," "at the end of the day"
  • "cutting-edge," "state-of-the-art," "next-level," "paradigm shift"
  • "harness the power," "seamlessly," "effortlessly," "elevate your"
  • "synergy / synergies," "leverage opportunities," "whether you're a seasoned"
  • "in conclusion"

Em-dashes banned everywhere except sign-off "- [Name]" which uses ASCII hyphen.

Brand-specific ban list:

  • "Babe / Queen / Boss-babe / Divine / Goddess / Manifest" overused. (The Academy uses "Babe" once in the brand name. Over-using it in copy reads pander.)
  • "Hustle / Grind / Pressure cooker / 60-hour weeks as a badge" (Soft Life register specifically rejects this framing.)
  • "I'll teach you my secrets" (under-respects the operator)
  • "Inner power / divine feminine awakening" (too soft-feminine, off-brand for the operator math layer)
  • "Just trust the process" (vague, off-brand)

Compliance ban list (FTC scope):

  • "Guaranteed income" / "Make $X in 30 days" / "Replace your salary in 90 days"
  • "Anyone can do this" / "Results typical" (these are FTC-watched phrases)
  • "Passive income" used without context. Jenn's brand uses "passive" but it's calibrated against her methodology framing. Copy must follow her register, not flatten "passive" into "set-it-and-forget-it"

3. Brand Pillars (Voice + Positioning)

Four pillars rotate across all surfaces. Each pillar has a register accent.

Pillar 1 · Corporate-To-$48M Track Record

Register accent: Methodology-rich. Receipt-driven. ADP / LocaliQ / Funnel Pop Media → $48M closed revenue arc is the credibility spine. Voice on this pillar reads like someone who knows the math because she ran the math.

Sample line: "After a decade at ADP, LocaliQ, and Funnel Pop Media, I closed $48M in 2025. The corporate stack is the foundation under everything I teach."

Pillar 2 · The Soft-Life Operating System

Register accent: Soft-life accent on top of operator math. The lifestyle is the outcome of the business design, not the business itself. Voice reads grounded, structured, intentional.

Sample line: "Soft Life isn't a vibe. It's a P&L. Built so the business funds the life, not the other way around."

Pillar 3 · Women-Only High-Ticket Sales Methodology

Register accent: Specific to the ICP. Acknowledges what works for women in high-ticket sales that the broader (bro-led) coaching world doesn't teach. Voice reads protective of the audience, not generic.

Sample line: "Women in high-ticket sales close differently than the bro-script teaches. Here's what closed $48M in 2025 instead."

Pillar 4 · The 6+7 Figure Passive Blueprint

Register accent: Methodology-curriculum register. The Blueprint is the operating system. Voice reads structured, step-driven, calibrated.

Sample line: "The 6+7 Figure Passive Blueprint runs on 6 steps. Step 1 is the one most women coaches skip."

6. Headline Construction Rules

For every headline drafted across the package:

  1. Open with the concrete claim or number ($48M, 63K, 10-year, 6+7 Figure)
  2. Pair it with the calibrating clause (the asymmetry, the gap, the leverage point)
  3. Avoid abstract benefit-stack adjectives ("transformative / breakthrough / next-level")
  4. Use em rule: zero em-dashes except in sign-off "- [Name]"
  5. Active verbs > passive constructions
  6. Soft-life accent applied only to lifestyle outcome, not methodology framing

7. Source Voice References

Mirror Jenn's actual voice tokens from her existing surfaces:

  • IG bio: "HIGH TICKET SALES COACH | Soft Life Income Coach for Women | 48M Sold in 2025"
  • Threads bio: "7 Figure Business Owner / Coach. Helping You Start + Scale Online. Let's All Escape The Matrix Together."
  • LinkedIn role: "CEO at CBA | Confidence Babe Academy" (since 2021)

The voice she uses publicly is the voice the copy should mirror. Read every prospect-facing surface against this register before publishing.

8. AG1 Voice On Hub-Page Surface

The hub page is the only surface in AG1's own voice (not Jenn's). The register on the hub:

  • Senior operator analysing her brand from the outside
  • Receipt-driven (numbers, audience tiers, conservative floors)
  • Calibrated to the female-founder ICP (no operator-bro cadence)
  • Hub language defaults to ICP-aware respect: "the audience capability is real, the ceiling is in distribution"
  • No "you should do X" prescriptive language. Frame is "here's what I see, here's what would compound."

9. Voice Audit Before Send

Before any prospect-facing copy ships:

  1. Read it aloud. Does it sound like Jenn would speak it (for landing / VSL / email) or like a senior operator analysing her brand (for hub / outreach)?
  2. Scan for banned phrases (use the lint script).
  3. Scan for em-dashes (zero tolerance outside sign-off).
  4. Scan for soft-feminine pander tokens overused ("babe / queen / divine").
  5. Scan for FTC-watched language (guaranteed income / typical results / passive without context).
  6. Scan for the corporate-to-$48M arc. Is the credibility spine carried through the copy? If absent, add it back.

If any check fails, fix before publishing.
